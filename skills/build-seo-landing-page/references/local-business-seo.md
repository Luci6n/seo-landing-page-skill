# Local Business SEO

Use this when the landing page is for a local shop, contractor, clinic, venue, restaurant, or service provider.

## Core Local Signals

- Exact business name
- Business category
- Address
- Phone or WhatsApp
- Opening hours
- Service areas
- Map link
- Google Business Profile
- Real photos
- Reviews

## Structured Data

Use the most specific applicable schema type:

- `LocalBusiness`
- `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`
- `HVACBusiness`
- `Store`
- `Restaurant`
- `MedicalBusiness`
- `ProfessionalService`

Include:

- `name`
- `url`
- `logo`
- `image`
- `telephone`
- `address`
- `geo`
- `openingHoursSpecification`
- `areaServed`
- `hasMap`
- `contactPoint`

## Google Business Profile

Recommended setup:

- Claim or create the profile.
- Use exact business name.
- Add website URL.
- Add phone/WhatsApp.
- Add opening hours.
- Add service areas.
- Add business category.
- Add real photos.
- Add services.
- Ask real customers for reviews.

Other local platforms may matter depending on the country and audience:

- Apple Business Connect
- Bing Places
- Facebook business page
- Local map providers or directories
- Industry-specific directories

## Domain Advice

Domain choice should follow the business market, not a fixed extension rule.

General guidance:

- Country-code domains can help local trust when the business serves one country.
- Formal business-oriented second-level domains may be useful in some markets, such as `.com.my`, `.com.sg`, `.co.uk`, or `.com.au`.
- `.com` is broadly trusted and useful for wider audiences.
- Newer extensions can work when they fit the brand, but check audience trust and renewal cost.

Examples:

- Malaysian local business: `.com.my`, `.my`, or `.com`.
- Singapore local business: `.com.sg`, `.sg`, or `.com`.
- UK business: `.co.uk`, `.uk`, or `.com`.
- Global SaaS or portfolio: often `.com`, but brand fit matters.

Pick a domain that is easy to say, type, remember, and renew.
